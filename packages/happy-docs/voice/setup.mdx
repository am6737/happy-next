Voice is configured in the Happy mobile app under **Settings > Voice**.

## Happy Voice (default)

Happy Voice is built into the app and works out of the box — no additional setup or registration required. Just tap the microphone icon in any session to start a voice conversation.

Happy Voice uses a voice gateway based on Volcengine (ByteDance Volcano Engine) RTC, with a Volc ASR → Doubao LLM → Volcano TTS pipeline. The hosted gateway is provided for all users by default.

### Voice and speech rate

You can personalize how Happy Voice sounds under **Settings > Voice**:

- **Voice** — Pick from a range of built-in voices. Tap a voice to preview it before selecting; the default voice is used until you choose another.
- **Speech rate** — A slider from slow to fast, with normal as the default. Adjust it to make spoken responses slower or quicker.

<Info>
The built-in voices are tuned for Chinese and English, so those two languages sound the most natural.
</Info>

### Settings sync

Your voice preferences — language, voice, speech rate, action confirmation, welcome message, and any self-hosted gateway settings — are stored in your end-to-end encrypted user settings and sync automatically across all your devices.

### Self-hosted customization

If you want full control over the voice pipeline — choosing your own STT, LLM, and TTS models, or keeping voice data on your own infrastructure — you can deploy your own Happy Voice gateway.

Requirements:
- A Volcengine (ByteDance Volcano Engine) RTC "AI agent" app — `VOLC_RTC_APP_ID` / `VOLC_RTC_APP_KEY` — plus IAM access keys (`VOLC_ACCESS_KEY_ID` / `VOLC_SECRET_ACCESS_KEY`) for the RTC OpenAPI
- Volcano big-model TTS credentials — `VOLC_TTS_APP_ID` / `VOLC_TTS_TOKEN`
- A `VOICE_AUTH_SECRET` shared with your Happy server, used to sign the short-lived bearer tokens the app exchanges for gateway access

The gateway runs a Volc seed ASR → Doubao LLM (via Ark) → Volcano big-model TTS pipeline; these providers are configured through the gateway's environment variables.

The Happy app discovers your gateway URL automatically from your Happy server's `/v1/app-config` — there is no Provider, Gateway URL, or Public Key to set in the app. Point your server at your deployed gateway and the app picks it up.

See [Self-Hosting](/advanced/self-hosting) for deployment instructions.
