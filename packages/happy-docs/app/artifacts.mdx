---
title: "Artifacts"
description: "Encrypted notes you can create, edit, and read on any device."
---

Artifacts are encrypted personal notes stored with Happy. Each artifact is a simple document with a title and a markdown body. Content is end-to-end encrypted, so only your devices can read it.

## Creating a note

Tap the **+** button on the Artifacts screen to start a new note. Give it a title, write the body in markdown, and save. A note needs content in at least one of the two fields to be saved.

## Viewing a note

Open any note from the list to read it. The title and last-updated date appear at the top, and the body is rendered as formatted markdown.

## Editing and deleting

From a note's detail screen you can:

- **Edit** — Update the title or body and save your changes.
- **Delete** — Remove the note permanently.

## Encryption

Every artifact is encrypted with its own key before it leaves your device, so the server never sees your note titles or contents in plaintext.
